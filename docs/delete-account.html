Delete your account & data

Applies to the CardLink app (package com.cardlink.app)

You can permanently delete your CardLink account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open CardLink and go to the Profile tab
  2. Tap the ⚙ Settings icon (top right)
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account
  4. Confirm twice — your account is deleted immediately

Option 2 — By email

If you can no longer access the app, email stefan.ivan334@gmail.com from the email address your account is registered with, using the subject “CardLink account deletion request”. We will delete the account and confirm within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (username, display name, bio, avatar, approximate location)
  • Your collection, binders, and wishlist
  • Your trades, reviews, chat threads and messages
  • Your feed posts and notifications
  • Photos you uploaded (card scans, chat images, avatar)
  • Your push notification tokens and analytics events tied to your account

Residual copies in database backups are purged on a rolling basis. Records we are legally required to keep (e.g. moderation reports you were involved in) may be retained in de-identified or minimal form.
