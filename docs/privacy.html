Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 July 2026

This policy explains what information CardLink (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and what control you have over it. The short version: we collect what the app needs to work as a social trading tool, we don’t sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible

CardLink is operated by its developer (contact: stefan.ivan334@gmail.com). For data-protection purposes, the developer is the data controller. If a company entity is established for CardLink, this section will be updated.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: email address, username, display name, and optionally a bio and avatar photo.
  • Your content: the cards in your collection (including condition, finish, and grading you enter), binders, wishlist, trade offers and their history, reviews, chat messages, and photos you upload (card scans, chat images, avatar).
  • Approximate location: with your permission, your device location rounded to roughly 1 km, plus a city/region name. It powers “near me” trading and feed ranking. Your approximate area (city and rounded coordinates) is visible to other users as part of your public profile; your precise location is never stored. You can decline the permission and set a location manually, or not at all.
  • Notifications token: a push token for your device so we can deliver notifications (messages, trade updates, follows, wishlist matches).
  • Usage analytics: basic in-app events (e.g. which screens are used, that a trade was proposed) stored in our own database. We do not use third-party analytics trackers.
  • Support messages: anything you send through the in-app feedback and report forms.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and related data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partners. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

4. Card scanning

When you scan a card with the camera, the photo is sent to our server, which uses Google Cloud Vision to read the card’s name and number so we can match it. Scan photos are used for this matching; photos you explicitly attach to your collection or chats are stored with your account.

5. Where your data lives & who processes it

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, storage, and server functions.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.
  • Google — AdMob advertising and Cloud Vision scanning (as described above).
  • Pokémon TCG API — card catalogue data and card images are fetched from public sources; your personal data is not sent to them.
  • Resend — sending account emails (confirmation codes, password reset).

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. We don’t sell your personal information to anyone.

6. Legal bases (EU/EEA users)

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), to keep the platform safe and improve it (legitimate interest), and — for optional things like location, notifications, and personalised ads — based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings or in the app.

7. How long we keep data

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, collection, binders, wishlist, trades, chats, posts, and uploaded photos are permanently removed (see Delete your account). Residual copies in backups and logs are purged on a rolling basis.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do directly in the app (Edit Profile, Settings → Delete account). For anything else, email stefan.ivan334@gmail.com. EU/EEA users also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database access is enforced row-by-row so users can only read and write what they’re allowed to. No system is perfectly secure — please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

CardLink is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we’ll update this page and note it in the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
